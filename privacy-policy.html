Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Home OS ("we", "our", or "us") is a household management app designed to help you organise tasks, bills, inventory, shopping lists, meal planning, and more. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use Home OS.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Home OS, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information: your name and email address.
  • Household data: tasks, bills, inventory items, shopping lists, meal history, and recipe preferences that you create or manage within the app.
  • Receipt data: images or text from receipts you scan to add items to your inventory.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain the Home OS service, including syncing household data between household members.
  • Power AI-driven recipe matching and recommendations based on your inventory and preferences.
  • Process receipt scans via the Claude API to extract item information for your inventory.
  • Improve the app experience and develop new features.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate Home OS:

  • Supabase: provides our database infrastructure and user authentication. Your account and household data is stored on Supabase servers. Supabase is SOC 2 compliant.
  • Anthropic (Claude API): powers receipt scanning and AI recipe matching. Receipt images and relevant household data may be sent to Anthropic for processing. Anthropic does not use this data to train their models.

4. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you wish to delete your data, you may request deletion at any time by contacting us, and we will remove your personal information from our systems within a reasonable timeframe.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct any inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Delete your personal data and account.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at jeff@homeos.app.

6. Data Security

We take data security seriously. All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Your data is stored in Supabase, which is SOC 2 compliant and employs industry-standard security practices including encryption at rest.

7. Children's Privacy

Home OS is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at jeff@homeos.app.
